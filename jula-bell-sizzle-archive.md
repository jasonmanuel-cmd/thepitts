# Jula Bell — Sizzle Reel Research Archive

Compiled from a deep-archive internet scrub (official site, Internet Archive, zines, press, film/TV databases, streaming platforms, Discogs-adjacent aggregators). Every entry includes a direct source link and media-type tag so clips/photos/audio can be pulled quickly for a sizzle reel.

**Subject:** Jula Bell — singer/bassist/autoharpist, LA punk fixture since 1986. Opera major at Boston University; voice studies at USC. Founding member of Bulimia Banquet (1986–1992, riot-grrrl precursor featured in *Not Bad for a Girl*), Bobsled (1992–), Nip Drivers (2004), Marc Spitz Freestyle (~2000), Miss Derringer (2004). Twin Peaks crew + on-screen cameos; 23D Films line producer; only female to duet with DEVO (per Mark Mothersbaugh); session/voice work across TV, film, and commercials.

## 6. Band context (for on-screen supers / captions)

- **Bulimia Banquet** (LA, 1986–1992): art-punk/hardcore/metal mash. Members incl. Ingrid Baumgart (Malicious Grind, Ingrate Souls, Razebrae), Jason Greenwood (PUI, TVTV$), Al Hansford (Omlits, Imperial Buttwizards, Bath Salt Zombiez), Greg Cameron (SWA, Punk Rock Zombies, Jeff Dahl), Dez Cadena (Black Flag/Flag/DC3), Nick Passiglia (Nip Drivers), Mia Ferraro (Spoon, Hole, Sapphic Musk), Travis Johnson (Final Conflict, Dickies, Dick Dale), Steve Dro (Angry Samoans, Moral Decay, Ill Repute, Fifi, Roman's Weirdos); Jennifer Precious Finch subbed 2 gigs. Toured US, Europe, Mexico, Canada. Flipside cover band. Riot-grrrl precursor. Featured in *Not Bad for a Girl*.
- **Bobsled** (1992–): Jula (bass/vox), Peter Tomlinson (Glue, Pedro, Muriel, Esther, El Vez), Steve Dro; girl-band era w/ Judy Cocuzza (Betty Blowtorch, Borax, Priss), Patricia Klein (Patsy, Blue Man Group), Sharon Needles (Butt Trumpet, Betty Blowtorch, Hells Belles), Mia Ferraro. Singles on Hell Yeah + Helter Skelter; LP on Tacklebox/Cargo. NW–Canada + SW tours with FYP. Featured in *Not Bad for a Girl*.
- **Nip Drivers** (Jula, 2004): hard/fast LA punk; Mike Webber lyrics. Mike died of a drug overdose months after she joined.
- **Marc Spitz Freestyle** (~2000): "driving rock with some country punk thrown in"; named for Olympic swimmer Mark Spitz (distinct from rock writer Marc Spitz).
- **Miss Derringer** (2004, 1st incarnation): autoharp + backup vox; w/ Liz McGrath (Tongue), Jeremy Szuder (Los Cincos), Mark Miller (Skeetertruck), Derek O'Brien (Adolescents/Social Distortion), Morgan Slade; Sympathy for the Record Industry single + "King James, Crown Royal and a Colt 45."
- **Earlier projects**: The Fags (1984–85, w/ Jennifer Finch), Minions of Parody (1985), Cactus Dick (KXLU DJ band), The Bob Drivers (1999–2000 acoustic, w/ Mike Webber), The Manzanita Sisters (acoustic w/ Mia Ferraro + Mark "washboard king"), Jula Bell & Eddie Muñoz Project.
